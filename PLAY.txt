PETAL GARDEN

On your Mac
-----------
Double-click index.html to open the Elm game in your browser. No installation
or internet connection is needed. Keep the elm folder, style.css and icon.svg
alongside it. The former standalone JavaScript game has been removed.

On your iPhone (same trusted Wi-Fi network)
------------------------------------------
1. On your Mac, open Terminal and run:
   cd ~/flower-garden
   python3 -m http.server 8765 --bind 0.0.0.0
2. Find your Mac's Wi-Fi IP address in System Settings > Wi-Fi > Details > TCP/IP.
3. On your iPhone, open Safari and visit http://YOUR-MAC-IP:8765
4. Leave the Terminal running while you play. Press Control-C to stop sharing.

This exposes only this folder, but any device able to reach your Mac on that
network could access it. Use a trusted home network; do not forward the port
on your router. A firewall or managed network may prevent the connection.
Playing over Wi-Fi requires your Mac to stay awake.

For access without the Mac, the folder can be hosted as a static website over
HTTPS. Nothing has been uploaded or published automatically.

How to play
-----------
Place the current flower on an empty patch. Match three or more outer petal
colours in a horizontal or vertical line. No swapping, gravity or timer.
Layered blooms reveal their inner colour after a match, potentially creating
a cascade. Crossed matches affect each flower once per cascade step.
Butterflies recolour outer petals. Spades move a flower to an empty patch.
Tools may be skipped, but flowers cannot.

Each level has a finite batch of pieces. The board carries into the next
level, with more colours and layered blooms. New gardens start with 12 stones.
At each level transition, the stone target rises to 14, 16 and 18 at levels
2, 3 and 4, then drops by one per level back to 12 at level 10.
Stones grow in fixed wedges from the corners along the edges, leaving the
central garden connected. Only empty patches in that level's border pattern
become stones; occupied positions are skipped, never replaced or moved inward.
Later levels retract the wedges in reverse order to reopen the board.
After level 14, layered blooms become more frequent, reaching 90% of generated
flowers at level 28. The stone border stays at 12.
The game ends when the current piece is a flower and there is no empty patch.
Previously saved gardens keep their layout until the next level transition.
Choose New garden to try the 12-stone opening immediately.

Scores: 10 points per matched flower per cascade step, multiplied by that
step's number. Completing a level adds 100 times the completed level number.
Progress and best score save locally in this browser where permitted.
Private browsing, clearing website data or changing the address/browser can
remove or separate your saved game. Mac and iPhone saves are independent.

About this version
------------------
This is an independent, Flowerz-inspired implementation with original SVG art
and synthesised sound. It does not include Microsoft's code, artwork or audio.
It is not an exact reproduction: original level layouts, scoring and piece
distribution could not be verified, so these are newly designed.

Development
-----------
The maintained source is in elm/src. Elm handles rules and UI; elm/interop.js
provides browser storage, dialogs and synthesised audio. The compiled elm.js
is required to run the Elm application in a browser; it is not a second game.
See elm/README.txt for build and test commands. Old JavaScript saves can still
be explicitly imported if they exist in this browser at the same origin.
